Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you send a form, we receive the details you enter — such as your name, email or phone number, and message. We use them only to reply to you and discuss your project.

How forms are delivered

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through Formspree, a third-party form service that processes them on our behalf.

WhatsApp and email

If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, your messages are handled by those services under their own privacy policies.

Approximate country

To show the right contact options, the site checks your approximate country from your internet connection. We never request GPS access, and we don’t store your IP address.

Stored on your device

Your browser remembers your language choice, and the detected country code for up to 24 hours. You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

No tracking

We don’t use advertising or analytics trackers, and our fonts are hosted on our own site. The site is hosted on Vercel and GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs.

Questions about your data? Email us at technologieswasl@gmail.com
